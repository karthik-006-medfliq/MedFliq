# MedFliq SEO Keyword Strategy and On-Page Structure

## Positioning

**MedFliq** is positioned as an AI clinical co-pilot for Indian outpatient clinics and hospitals. Its SEO should focus on high-intent searches related to clinical AI, AI medical scribes, voice documentation, OPD workflows, and hospital workflow automation.

## Primary SEO Goals

- Rank for India-specific clinical AI and AI medical scribe searches
- Generate demo requests from hospitals, clinics, and doctors
- Build topical authority around clinical documentation and workflow automation
- Differentiate MedFliq from generic transcription and global AI-scribe products

## Internal Linking Rules

- Link each blog article to one relevant product or solution page.
- Link the AI medical scribe page to voice assistant, clinical documentation, clinics, and hospitals pages.
- Link hospital workflow pages to integrations, security, and case studies.
- Use descriptive anchor text such as `AI medical scribe for Indian doctors` rather than generic text such as `click here`.
- Avoid placing multiple pages in competition for the same primary keyword.

## Schema Markup

Implement schema only when the information is accurate and visible on the page.

- `Organization` on the homepage
- `SoftwareApplication` on product and solution pages
- `FAQPage` on pages with visible FAQ sections
- `BreadcrumbList` across the website
- `Article` or `BlogPosting` on resource pages
- `Review` or `AggregateRating` only where genuine, eligible, and visible reviews exist

## Technical and Trust Checklist

- Use one clear H1 per page.
- Add a unique title tag and meta description to every indexable page.
- Keep URLs concise, lowercase, and keyword-focused.
- Ensure fast mobile performance and usable Core Web Vitals.
- Add descriptive image alt text where images add meaning.
- Create and submit XML sitemap files through Google Search Console.
- Use canonical tags to prevent duplicate-content issues.
- Publish Privacy Policy, Terms, Contact, and Security pages.
- Do not make unsupported claims about diagnosis, accuracy, regulatory compliance, patient-data security, integrations, language support, or time saved.
- If applicable, explain clinician review and approval rather than implying autonomous medical decisions.

## Important Notes

- Use competitor analysis to understand market language and content gaps, not to copy their content or brands.
- Focus first on bottom-of-funnel service and solution pages before publishing a large volume of blog content.
- Target local modifiers such as `India`, `Indian doctors`, `OPD`, `clinics`, and `hospitals` where they truthfully describe MedFliq's offering.
- Validate search volume, difficulty, regional demand, and keyword variants with Google Search Console, Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush, or similar SEO tools before final prioritization.
